3. Risk disclosure

Trading OTC binary options, forex and crypto carries a high risk of losing all of your capital. It is not suitable for most people. Confidence and match scores are model outputs, not guarantees.

Nothing displayed by the engine — confidence percentage, match score, direction, entry time or historical win rate — is a promise, prediction or assurance of profit. A 99% confidence reading can still lose. You must never trade money you cannot afford to lose, never trade with borrowed funds, and never increase risk on the basis of a score.

OTC (over-the-counter) instruments are priced by the platform offering them, not by a public exchange. Their quotes can differ from the underlying interbank market and are generated here by a synthetic liquidity model for demonstration of the interface.

4. Data sources, delays and accuracy

  • Forex — spot rates and 120 days of daily closes from public central-bank and community feeds (European Central Bank via Frankfurter; currency-api via jsDelivr).
  • Crypto — prices, 24h statistics and 1m–15m candles from public Binance market endpoints, with CoinGecko as a fallback.
  • Indian market — rupee quotes derived from the live USD/INR rate and the crypto feeds above.
  • OTC pairs — generated by the local synthetic liquidity model, because OTC instruments have no public order book.

Public feeds may be delayed, rate-limited, geo-restricted, interrupted or simply wrong. Where a feed cannot be reached the terminal falls back to a local model and labels the feed accordingly. Always verify a price on your broker's platform before acting on it.
